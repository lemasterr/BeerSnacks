What we collect

This site is a catalogue of our shops. We do not ask for your name, phone or any other personal data and sell nothing online. Orders are placed through Wolt and Bolt Food under their own terms.

What is stored in your browser

  • your chosen language and theme (light/dark);
  • your confirmation that you are 18 or older;
  • a note that you closed the cookie notice.

These stay only on your device (localStorage) and are needed for the site to work. We use no advertising or analytics cookies.

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical data (such as IP address) for security. The map pictures (© OpenStreetMap contributors) are stored on our own site. External links (Wolt, Bolt Food, Google Maps, social networks) open under those services' terms.
