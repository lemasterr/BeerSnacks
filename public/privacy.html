What data we process

This site is a catalogue of our shops and an online shop with in-store pickup. When you place an order, we process your name, phone number, email (if you enter it), comment, the order contents and payment method. Purpose: fulfilling the order and contacting you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and accounting obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)). To prevent abuse we store a marker derived from your IP address (not the IP address itself) with the order. Order data is kept for 7 years as required by the Accounting Act. You can also order through Wolt and Bolt Food under their own terms.

Technical data

When the site is shown, our host Cloudflare processes technical data (IP address, browser, page address) to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). We do not store this data ourselves; Cloudflare keeps it for a short time under its own terms.

Visit statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which shows only aggregate figures (number of visits, most viewed pages). It uses no cookies and does not identify people. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

What is stored in your browser

Stored on your device (localStorage): your chosen language and theme, your age confirmation, a note that you closed the cookie notice, the animation setting, your cart, the name, phone and email you last entered (so you don't have to type them again) and links to your orders. They stay in your browser only and you can delete them at any time in the browser settings. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Processor and data transfers

Hosting, content delivery, the order database and statistics: Cloudflare, Inc. Data may be processed outside the EU (USA); transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

External links

Links to Wolt, Bolt Food, Google Maps and social networks open under those services’ terms and privacy policies.

Your rights

You may ask what data about you is processed, request correction or deletion, restrict processing and object to it. Write to us by email. If you believe your rights have been violated, you can contact the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (www.aki.ee, info@aki.ee).
